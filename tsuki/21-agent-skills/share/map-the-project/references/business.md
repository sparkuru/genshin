# Business Module

Use this module for a business, product, initiative, organization, market-facing process, or operating model. Focus on value creation and the conditions that make the work viable.

## Discover

- Identify customers, users, sponsors, operators, partners, and decision owners.
- Locate the value proposition, problem addressed, offer, channel, revenue or funding model, costs, and constraints.
- Trace the operating cycle from demand or input through delivery, outcomes, measurement, learning, and adjustment.
- Distinguish observed performance from plans, narratives, targets, and anecdotes.

## Map

Use the relevant dimensions:

- customer problem, segment, and value proposition;
- products or services, channels, and experience;
- capabilities, people, suppliers, and operating process;
- economics, incentives, capacity, and critical assumptions;
- metrics, feedback loops, risks, and external conditions.

## Assess

Evaluate coherence: whether the customer problem, offer, delivery capability, economics, and metrics reinforce one another. State missing evidence such as unit economics, customer behavior, capacity, or decision rights rather than assuming it exists.
