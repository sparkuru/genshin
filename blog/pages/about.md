## A deliberately small beginning

This foundation keeps authored content separate from its presentation. Posts and
pages stay useful as plain Markdown and as build-time HTML.

Future presentations can change how the site looks without changing the source
contract established here.

## What remains constant

Each public document keeps a direct route, a sequential heading structure, and
complete content when browser JavaScript is unavailable.
